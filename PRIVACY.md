# Privacy policy: research-toolkit

Last updated: 2026-11-24

research-toolkit is an open-source plugin of instructions for Claude. The author runs no server and no service for it, and receives, collects, stores and shares no data from anyone who uses it.

It sends your search queries to Exa through the Exa connector (MCP server) that the plugin declares. Exa's handling of those queries is covered by Exa's own privacy policy: https://exa.ai/privacy-policy. Research notes it writes are saved to your own workspace.

Anything you send to Claude is handled under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy, not by this plugin's author.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/dougkeiller/research-toolkit/issues.
